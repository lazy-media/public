### Data we collect

#### 1) Account and authentication data

When you log in to Lazy Media services, we may collect:

* Email address.
* Username and display name.
* Profile image (if provided by your identity provider).
* Authentik user ID and group memberships.
* Login events (timestamp, success/failure, source, IP address).

We commonly support social login via Authentik.

Examples are documented here:

* [Plex & Twitch - OAuth Authentication, Enrollment, & User Group Assignment Setup](../../authentik/federation-and-social-login/plex-and-twitch-social-login-setup.md)
* [Discord - OAuth Authentication, Enrollment, & User Group Assignment Setup](../../authentik/federation-and-social-login/discord.md)

If you use Discord login, we may verify:

* Discord server (guild) membership.
* Discord role membership.

That behavior is described in the Discord setup guide above.

#### 2) Content you upload or create in apps

Different apps store different user content.

Examples include:

* Photos, videos, and metadata in [Immich](../../immich.md).
* Files, calendars, and collaboration content in [Nextcloud](../../nextcloud.md).
* Documents and OCR/metadata in [Paperless-NGX](../../paperless-ngx.md).
* Messages and attachments in [Rocket-Chat Server](../../rocketchat-server.md).
* Password vault items you store in [Vaultwarden](../../vaultwarden.md).
* Posts, media, and profile data in [Mastodon](../../mastodon.md).
* Tasks and project content in [Vikunja](../../vikunja.md).
* Resume content and exports in [Reactive Resume](../../reactive-resume.md).
* Scheduling details you submit in [Cal.com Appointment & Scheduling](../../cal.com-appointment-and-scheduling.md).

#### 3) Technical and security data

We may collect technical data to run services safely:

* IP address and user agent.
* Request timestamps and URLs.
* Basic device and browser details.
* Error logs and performance metrics.

This data helps with abuse prevention and troubleshooting.

#### 4) Communications and support

If you request access or support, we may collect:

* The information you submit in our forms.
* Emails you send to us, and our replies.
* Messages you send in our Discord support channels.

Server access is described here:

* [Joining Lazy Media's Server](./)

#### 5) Donations and payments

If you donate or sponsor, payment data is handled by the payment platform.

We may receive limited data like your name, handle, and donation amount.

See:

* [Donations & Sponsors](../donations-and-sponsors.md)

### How we use your data

We use your data to:

* Provide and operate the services.
* Authenticate you and enforce permissions.
* Protect services from abuse and fraud.
* Communicate with you about access and support.
* Maintain backups and recover from incidents.

We do not sell your personal data.

### Legal bases (if you’re in the EEA/UK)

We process data under these bases:

* Contract: to provide the service you request.
* Legitimate interests: security, abuse prevention, and reliability.
* Consent: for optional features where applicable.

### How data is shared

We share data only when needed to provide the service.

#### Identity providers (social login)

If you sign in via a third party (like Plex or Discord), they process your login too.

They may share limited profile data with Authentik.

See the Authentik social login docs:

* [Plex & Twitch - OAuth Authentication, Enrollment, & User Group Assignment Setup](../../authentik/federation-and-social-login/plex-and-twitch-social-login-setup.md)
* [Discord - OAuth Authentication, Enrollment, & User Group Assignment Setup](../../authentik/federation-and-social-login/discord.md)

#### Infrastructure and security providers

We may use third-party providers to operate and secure the services, including:

* Reverse proxy, DNS, and TLS services.
* DDoS mitigation and firewalling.

Where used, Lazy Media uses Cloudflare in a standard configuration.

This includes DNS and Cloudflare edge security features (for example, SSL/TLS settings).

Lazy Media hosted services do **not** use Cloudflare Tunnel or Cloudflare Zero Trust.

An example of our Cloudflare settings is documented in the “Cloudflare Setup” section here:

* [Authentik Installation](../../authentik/authentik-installation.md)

We may also implement additional infrastructure and security controls that are not listed in this policy.

#### Payments

Payments and recurring support are processed by their platforms.

See:

* [Donations & Sponsors](../donations-and-sponsors.md)

### Cookies and session storage

Most Lazy Media services use cookies for:

* Login sessions.
* CSRF protection.
* Preferences.

If you block cookies, some services may not work.

### Data retention

We keep data only as long as needed for:

* Operating the service.
* Security and abuse prevention.
* Legal or compliance obligations (if applicable).

When you delete content in an app, it may remain in backups for a limited time.

### Security

We use reasonable security controls.

That includes access controls and encrypted transport (HTTPS).

No system is perfectly secure.

You should use strong passwords and enable 2FA where available.

### Your choices and rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to:

* Access your personal data.
* Correct inaccurate data.
* Delete your account or content.
* Export your data (when the app supports it).
* Object to or restrict processing in certain cases.

To request account deletion or a data export, contact us.

### Children’s privacy

These services are not intended for children under 13.

Do not use them if you are under 13.

### Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as services change.

We will update the “Last updated” date.
